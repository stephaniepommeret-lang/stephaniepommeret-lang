    <p><strong>2020 - Alliance Française of São Tomé</strong></p>
    <p>
        <strong>Embroidered and sewn maps, in wax and bazin, 120 × 80 cm</strong>
    </p>
    <br/>
    <h3>World Map in Wax Patchwork - São Tomé</h3>
    <p>
        Invited by the <a class="highlight-link" href="https://ga.ambafrance.org/Alliance-Francaise-a-Sao-Tome-et-Principe-1640" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alliance Française of São Tomé</a> (Gulf of Guinea), I
        collaborated with local
        seamstresses and embroiderers. They assemble pieces of wax fabric into precious re-embroidered
        patchwork.
    </p>
    <p>
        This map uses Fuller's projection, which represents the Earth on an unfolded polyhedron, offering an
        alternative vision of the world.
    </p>
    <p>
        Collaboration with Judith Sylva, Lauriane Chanel and the Alliance Française team of São Tomé.
    </p>
