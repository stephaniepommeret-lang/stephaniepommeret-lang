<div class="text-block">
    <p>
        This project came to life thanks to the <a class="highlight-link" href="https://www.limagequiparleblog.org/huis-clos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Association L’Image qui parle</a>, a cultural
        association led by Patricia Le Calvez. Rooted in the Paimpol area, it is supported by institutions
        such as the DRAC Bretagne, the CIAS of Guingamp-Paimpol Agglomération, the Departmental Council of
        Côtes-d’Armor, the CAF of Côtes-d’Armor and the town of Paimpol, along with essential partnerships
        with the Guingamp-Paimpol gendarmerie and the Association Maison de l’Argoat. It is a collaborative
        and social approach, bringing together poetry, photography, installation, sound collection and dance!
    </p>
</div>
<div class="text-block">
    <p>
       These creations were born from the need to make women's voices visible in the common space.
       These creations are the means used to account for the violence suffered by women on a daily basis as well as in their intimacy, by offering them a place of listening and visibility.
    </p>
    <p>
        These texts, photographs, podcasts and installation were created from testimonies of women from Paimpol and its agglomeration. The texts are hybrid, between narration and poetry, like an analogy.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="quote">
    « These words, these meetings with women victims nourish my work, it is my raw material. From this harvest of words, I take snippets. I rework these stories through the artistic prism. I wish to act at my level as an artist, to give voice to women victims. With art, I found a way to externalize my thoughts and bring the repressed to the surface. »
</div>

<div class="text-block">
    <p>
        The installation presents about twenty contemporary dresses suspended at different heights. The
        dresses feature red velvet pockets, symbolizing intimate and collective wounds, inside which the
        texts are placed.
    </p>
</div>
<div class="text-block">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 4rem;">Danced Performance: SWEET LURE - SWEET HOUR</h3>
    <p>
        Sylvie Le Quéré, choreographer and dancer of the company <a class="highlight-link" href="https://www.ciegregoireandco.fr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Grégoire and Co</a>, invites these women to set themselves in motion together, in dance to share, to reclaim through the body the transformation of pain becoming sweetness.
    </p>
