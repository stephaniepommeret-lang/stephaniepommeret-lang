<section class="intro">
    <h1 class="project-title">Bio / CV</h1>

    <div class="bio-text">
        <p>Stéphanie Pommeret, visual artist, Saint-Brieuc, Côtes d'Armor, Brittany.</p>
        <p>"Though coming from a poetic approach, Stéphanie Pommeret’s work is based on social issues that are
        crucial to the choice of the techniques and material being used."</p>
    </div>
