        "Though coming from a poetic approach, Stéphanie Pommeret’s work is based on social issues that are
        crucial to the choice of the techniques and material being used. This brings about an interaction
        pushing the artist to review experimentations constantly. To simple technical gestures, she
        associates ordinary materials that are turned away from their daily use and it’s in its diversion
        that art comes out."
